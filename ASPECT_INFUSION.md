# Aspect Infusion System

## Overview
The Aspect Infusion system is a unique magical enhancement feature that allows players to temporarily infuse themselves with the power of discovered aspects. By consuming crystallized aspect infusions, players gain themed magical buffs accompanied by spectacular particle effects.

## What are Aspect Infusion Crystals?

Aspect Infusion Crystals are consumable items that embody the pure essence of a specific aspect. When consumed, they grant the player aspect-themed magical effects for 60 seconds, along with a visual display of colorful particles.

## Features

- **16 Unique Infusions**: One for each aspect (6 primal + 10 compound)
- **Themed Buffs**: Each aspect grants effects that match its theme
- **Visual Flair**: Beautiful particle effects in aspect-specific colors
- **Stackable**: Can be carried in bulk for extended adventures
- **Enchantment Glint**: All infusion crystals shimmer with magical energy

## How to Use

1. Obtain an Aspect Infusion Crystal (available in Creative Mode)
2. Hold the crystal in your hand
3. Right-click and hold to consume (1.6 seconds)
4. Enjoy your aspect-themed buffs for 60 seconds!

## Obtaining Aspect Infusions

Currently, Aspect Infusion Crystals are available in Creative Mode under the ThaumVibe creative tab. They appear between the aspect shards and middle-game materials.

**Future Plans** (when crafting is implemented):
- Craft at the Infusion Altar using aspect shards + vis crystals
- Transmute in the Crucible with essentia
- Rare drops from magical creatures
- Rewards from research completion

## Tips & Strategies

1. **Stack Effects**: Different infusions don't conflict - you can use multiple!
2. **Prepare for Adventure**: Consume infusions before dangerous activities
3. **Aspect Synergy**: Combine infusions that complement each other (e.g., Motus + Lux for fast cave exploration)
4. **Emergency Healing**: Victus infusion provides instant saturation + regeneration
5. **Boss Preparation**: Terra, Metallum, or Vitreus for maximum survivability
6. **Speed Mining**: Potentia + Motus = ultra-fast mining and movement

## Particle Effects

Each infusion creates a stunning visual display:
1. **Spiral Animation**: 50 particles spiral upward around the player
2. **Aspect-Themed**: Particles match the aspect's theme (flames for fire, hearts for life, etc.)
3. **Enchantment Burst**: Final burst of enchantment particles at player location
4. **Sound Effect**: Level-up sound plays when consumed

## Technical Details

- **Duration**: 60 seconds (1200 ticks)
- **Consumption Time**: 1.6 seconds (32 ticks)
- **Animation**: Eating/drinking animation
- **Creative Mode**: Not consumed in creative mode
- **Stackable**: Yes (default stack size: 64)

## Balancing

The system is balanced around:
- **Duration**: 60 seconds is enough for one major activity
- **No Crafting (Yet)**: Limited to creative/admin spawning for now
- **Trade-offs**: Perditio includes negative effects as a balance
- **Stacking Allowed**: Players can layer effects for creative combinations
- **Theme Appropriate**: Each aspect gives effects that make thematic sense
