# Tekkit Integration - Magitech for ThaumVibe

## Overview
ThaumVibe now includes **magitech** features inspired by the legendary Tekkit modpack. This integration combines Thaumcraft's mystical magic with Tekkit's industrial automation, creating a unique fusion of enchantment and engineering.

## What is Tekkit?
Tekkit was one of the most popular Minecraft modpacks ever created, combining mods like:
- **BuildCraft**: Pipes, quarries, and automation
- **IndustrialCraft**: Power generation, machines, and EU energy
- **Thermal Expansion**: Advanced processing and RF power
- **RedPower**: Logic circuits and frame motors

Tekkit revolutionized Minecraft by adding industrial-scale automation, power networks, and complex machinery.

## New Magitech Content

### Aspects
ThaumVibe adds two new aspects inspired by Tekkit's mechanical nature:

#### Machina (Machine)
- **Components**: Motus + Metallum
- **Represents**: Machines, automation, and mechanical devices
- **Color**: #8080A0 (Steel Blue)
- **Used in**: All magitech machinery and automation

#### Instrumentum (Tool)
- **Components**: Metallum + Ordo
- **Represents**: Tools, instruments, and precision devices
- **Color**: #A0A0C0 (Light Steel)
- **Used in**: Crafting components and specialized tools

### Items (5 Magitech Items)

#### 1. Essentia Tube
**Purpose**: Transport essentia between devices
**Inspired by**: BuildCraft pipes and Thermal Dynamics itemducts
**Use Cases**:
- Connect alchemical furnaces to storage
- Automate essentia delivery to infusion altars
- Create complex essentia networks

#### 2. Vis Conduit
**Purpose**: Distribute Vis power across your base
**Inspired by**: IndustrialCraft EU cables and Thermal Expansion conduits
**Use Cases**:
- Connect generators to machines
- Create centralized power systems
- Share Vis between multiple devices

#### 3. Arcane Levitator
**Purpose**: Move items using magical levitation
**Inspired by**: Pneumatic tubes and conveyor belts
**Use Cases**:
- Transport items vertically
- Sort and distribute materials
- Create item delivery systems

#### 4. Vis Battery
**Purpose**: Store large amounts of Vis for later use
**Inspired by**: Energy storage blocks (MFE, MFSU, Energy Cells)
**Use Cases**:
- Buffer power for high-demand devices
- Store excess Vis generation
- Provide emergency backup power

#### 5. Golem Animation Core
**Purpose**: The heart of a magical golem servant
**Inspired by**: Thaumcraft golems meet Tekkit automation
**Use Cases**:
- Create workers for repetitive tasks
- Automate harvesting and gathering
- Handle crafting and sorting

### Blocks (5 Magitech Blocks)

#### 1. Thaumic Generator
**Purpose**: Convert essentia into Vis power
**Inspired by**: Generators, dynamos, and EU producers
**Features**:
- Burns essentia for continuous Vis generation
- Can be chained for more power
- Outputs to Vis conduit networks

#### 2. Essentia Centrifuge
**Purpose**: Separate compound aspects into components
**Inspired by**: IndustrialCraft centrifuge and Thermal centrifugal separator
**Features**:
- Breaks down compound aspects
- Extracts pure primal aspects
- Enables advanced alchemy

#### 3. Arcane Quarry
**Purpose**: Automatically mine resources in an area
**Inspired by**: BuildCraft quarry
**Features**:
- Mines blocks in a defined area
- Powered by Vis
- Can be upgraded with speed/fortune
- Outputs to connected storage

#### 4. Vis Relay
**Purpose**: Extend and boost Vis network range
**Inspired by**: EU transformers and RF relays
**Features**:
- Extends conduit network reach
- Amplifies Vis transmission
- Reduces power loss over distance
- Can be chained for long distances

#### 5. Golem Workbench
**Purpose**: Craft and program magical golems
**Inspired by**: Assembly tables and programming interfaces
**Features**:
- Combine golem cores with bodies
- Program golem tasks and behaviors
- Upgrade golem capabilities
- Manage golem fleets

### Research Category
A new **Magitech** research category has been added:
- **Name**: "Industrial Magic"
- **Focus**: Automation, power networks, and mechanical magic
- **Prerequisites**: Mid-to-late game Thaumaturgy knowledge
- **Unlocks**: All magitech items and blocks

## Progression Path

### Prerequisites
Before accessing magitech, players must:
1. Complete basic Thaumaturgy research
2. Build an Infusion Altar
3. Discover the Machina and Instrumentum aspects
4. Research intermediate alchemy

### Early Magitech (First Steps)
1. Research the Magitech category
2. Craft your first Thaumic Generator
3. Create Vis Conduits for power distribution
4. Build a basic Vis network with a Vis Battery

### Mid Magitech (Automation)
1. Construct an Essentia Centrifuge
2. Set up Essentia Tubes for automated alchemy
3. Create your first Golem Animation Core
4. Build a Golem Workbench
5. Deploy golems for simple tasks

### Advanced Magitech (Industrial Scale)
1. Build an Arcane Quarry for automated mining
2. Create a large-scale Vis power grid with Relays
3. Use Arcane Levitators for complex item routing
4. Manage multiple golems for different tasks
5. Integrate magitech with traditional Thaumcraft systems

## Design Philosophy

### Balance
Magitech is powerful but expensive:
- **Resource Cost**: Requires rare magical materials
- **Research Requirement**: Late-game progression gating
- **Vis Consumption**: Power-hungry devices
- **Complexity**: Requires planning and infrastructure

### Synergy with Traditional Thaumcraft
Magitech doesn't replace traditional magic—it enhances it:
- **Crucibles** can be automated with golems
- **Infusion Altars** can receive automated essentia delivery
- **Wands** can be recharged from Vis networks
- **Research** still requires personal involvement

### Optional Gameplay
Players can choose their path:
- **Pure Mage**: Traditional Thaumcraft, all manual
- **Hybrid**: Some automation, mostly manual
- **Industrialist**: Full magitech, maximum automation

## Tips for Magitech Players

### From Tekkit Veterans
1. **Start Small**: Build a basic power network before scaling up
2. **Centralize Power**: Use Vis Batteries as buffers
3. **Plan Your Layout**: Conduits have limited range without Relays
4. **Upgrade Gradually**: Don't try to automate everything at once
5. **Monitor Resources**: Vis generation requires continuous essentia

### From Thaumcraft Experts
1. **Don't Forget Flux**: Magitech still generates flux
2. **Aspect Discovery**: Scan all magitech items for new aspects
3. **Research First**: Unlock upgrades before building infrastructure
4. **Balance Power**: Don't neglect traditional magic
5. **Combine Systems**: Use both automation and manual crafting

### General Advice
1. **Experiment**: Try different automation setups
2. **Document**: Keep notes on your network layouts
3. **Optimize**: Find the most efficient configurations
4. **Share**: Learn from other players' designs
5. **Have Fun**: The journey is as important as the destination

## Lore and Worldbuilding

### The Discovery of Magitech
*"For centuries, thaumaturges believed magic and machinery to be incompatible—that the rigid order of gears and pistons could not coexist with the fluid chaos of Vis and essentia. But a new generation of researchers, inspired by ancient texts and foreign innovations, has proven otherwise. By channeling magical energy through precisely-crafted conduits and programming animated constructs with crystallized intent, we have birthed a new discipline: Magitech, the marriage of mysticism and mechanism."*

—Excerpt from "The Magitech Manifesto" by Archmagister Tekk

### The Magitech Revolution
Where traditional thaumaturges work alone in towers, magitech engineers build factories. Where old-school alchemists perform rituals by candlelight, modern magicians deploy networks of golems and generators. The Magitech Revolution doesn't replace magical tradition—it amplifies it, allowing a single skilled practitioner to accomplish what once required an entire circle of mages.

### Ethical Considerations
Not all thaumaturges approve of magitech. Critics argue:
- **Loss of Craft**: Automation removes the personal touch
- **Flux Pollution**: Industrial-scale magic creates more flux
- **Dependency**: Relying on machines makes mages weak
- **Hubris**: Combining magic and technology invites disaster

Proponents counter:
- **Efficiency**: Why waste time on repetitive tasks?
- **Accessibility**: Automation makes magic available to more people
- **Innovation**: New techniques drive magical advancement
- **Sustainability**: Better resource management with networks

The debate continues, but one thing is certain: magitech has changed Thaumaturgy forever.
