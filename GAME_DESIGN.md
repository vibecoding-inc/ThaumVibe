# ThaumVibe - Thaumcraft Core Concepts

## Overview
ThaumVibe recreates the magical mod Thaumcraft for Minecraft 1.21.1. This document explains all the core gameplay mechanics and systems.

## 1. Aspects - The Foundation of Magic

### What are Aspects?
Aspects are fundamental magical elements that make up everything in the world. Every item, block, and entity contains various aspects.

### Primal Aspects (Cannot be broken down further)
1. **Aer (Air)** - Represents air, flight, and the sky
2. **Terra (Earth)** - Represents earth, stone, and the ground
3. **Ignis (Fire)** - Represents fire, heat, and destruction
4. **Aqua (Water)** - Represents water, fluidity, and life
5. **Ordo (Order)** - Represents structure, organization, and law
6. **Perditio (Entropy)** - Represents chaos, destruction, and decay

### Compound Aspects (Made from combining Primal Aspects)
1. **Vacuos (Void)** = Aer + Perditio - Emptiness and vacuum
2. **Lux (Light)** = Aer + Ignis - Light and illumination
3. **Motus (Motion)** = Aer + Ordo - Movement and change
4. **Gelum (Ice)** = Ignis + Perditio - Cold and freezing
5. **Vitreus (Crystal)** = Terra + Ordo - Glass and crystals
6. **Metallum (Metal)** = Terra + Ordo - Metals and ores
7. **Victus (Life)** = Aqua + Terra - Living things
8. **Mortuus (Death)** = Aqua + Perditio - Death and undeath
9. **Potentia (Energy)** = Ordo + Ignis - Power and energy
10. **Praecantatio (Magic)** = Vacuos + Potentia - Pure magical energy

## 2. Vis - Magical Energy

### What is Vis?
Vis is the magical energy that powers Thaumcraft devices. It flows through the world and can be stored in wands and other magical tools.

### Vis Storage
- Wands can store up to 100 Vis
- Vis recharges over time at a rate of 1 per tick
- Using magical abilities consumes Vis
- Running out of Vis prevents magical actions

### Using Vis
- Crafting at the Arcane Workbench requires Vis
- Powering magical devices
- Casting spells and magical effects

## 3. Research System

### Research Categories
1. **Basics** - Basic Thaumaturgy and getting started
2. **Alchemy** - Alchemical transmutation and essentia
3. **Artifice** - Magical item creation and golems
4. **Thaumaturgy** - Advanced magical knowledge
5. **Eldritch** - Forbidden knowledge (dangerous but powerful)

### How Research Works
1. Use the **Thaumometer** to scan items, blocks, and entities
2. Scanning discovers aspects and grants research progress
3. Complete research at the **Research Table**
4. Research requires specific aspects to be discovered
5. Completing research unlocks new recipes and abilities
6. Research is tracked in the **Thaumonomicon** (your research book)

## 4. Progression Path

### Early Game - Discovery (First Day)
**Objective**: Learn magic exists and gather basic materials

**Steps**:
1. Find Crystal Shards in caves (Air, Fire, Water, Earth, Order, Entropy)
2. Build a Crucible (cauldron for alchemy)
3. Create your first Thaumometer
4. Scan everything to discover aspects
5. Build a Research Table
6. Craft Salis Mundus (magical crafting catalyst)

**Unlocks**: Basic understanding of aspects

### Early-Mid Game - Establishment (Days 2-5)
**Objective**: Set up your magical workshop

**Steps**:
1. Build an Arcane Workbench
2. Craft your first Wand
3. Research basic Thaumaturgy
4. Mine Cinnabar and Amber Ores
5. Complete fundamental research

**Unlocks**: Ability to craft basic magical items

### Middle Game - Mastery (Days 5-15)
**Objective**: Master alchemy and infusion

**Steps**:
1. Build an Infusion Altar
2. Construct an Alchemical Furnace
3. Create an Essentia Smeltery
4. Research alchemical transmutation
5. Find and cultivate Shimmerleaf
6. Harvest Greatwood and Silverwood
7. Craft Thaumium equipment
8. Create Nitor (eternal flame)
9. Produce Alchemical Brass

**Unlocks**: 
- Thaumium tools and armor
- Complex magical crafting
- Essentia manipulation

### Late Game - Transcendence (Days 15+)
**Objective**: Delve into forbidden knowledge

**Steps**:
1. Research Eldritch knowledge
2. Craft Void Metal equipment
3. Create Primordial Pearls
4. Build Eldritch Obelisks
5. Obtain the Eldritch Eye
6. Monitor sanity with Sanity Checker
7. Manage Flux with Flux Scrubbers

**Unlocks**:
- Most powerful magical items
- Dimension manipulation
- Mastery over all aspects

**Warnings**:
- Eldritch research can have consequences
- Too much forbidden knowledge affects sanity
- Flux pollution can corrupt the world

## 5. Key Items and Their Uses

### Essential Tools
- **Thaumonomicon**: Your magical encyclopedia, tracks all research
- **Thaumometer**: Right-click to scan objects and discover aspects
- **Wand**: Stores Vis, used for all magical crafting

### Basic Materials
- **Salis Mundus**: "Magic salt" used to activate magical blocks
- **Crystal Shards**: Found naturally, contain primal aspects
- **Vis Crystals**: Concentrated magical energy storage

### Advanced Materials
- **Alchemical Brass**: Magical alloy for advanced crafting
- **Thaumium**: Magical metal stronger than iron
- **Nitor**: Eternal magical light source
- **Quicksilver**: Liquid magic essence

### Endgame Materials
- **Void Metal**: Metal from the void dimension
- **Primordial Pearl**: Essence of creation
- **Eldritch Eye**: Allows perception of hidden things
- **Sanity Checker**: Monitors mental stability

## 6. Key Blocks and Their Uses

### Early Game Stations
- **Crucible**: Alchemical cauldron for transmuting items
- **Research Table**: Where you complete research
- **Arcane Workbench**: Crafting table that uses Vis

### Middle Game Stations
- **Infusion Altar**: Multi-block structure for complex infusions
- **Alchemical Furnace**: Smelts items while extracting essentia
- **Essentia Smeltery**: Breaks down items into pure aspects

### Late Game Stations
- **Eldritch Obelisk**: Portal to eldritch dimensions
- **Flux Scrubber**: Removes magical pollution from the area

### Natural Blocks
- **Cinnabar Ore**: Source of Quicksilver
- **Amber Ore**: Contains preserved magical essence
- **Shimmerleaf**: Glowing magical plant
- **Greatwood Log**: Large magical trees
- **Silverwood Log**: Pure magical trees that repel flux

## 7. Gameplay Loop

1. **Explore** → Find magical materials and ores
2. **Scan** → Use Thaumometer to discover aspects
3. **Research** → Unlock new recipes at Research Table
4. **Craft** → Create magical items at Arcane Workbench
5. **Advance** → Build better magical stations
6. **Repeat** → Continue to more powerful magic

## 8. Tips for New Thaumaturges

1. Scan everything! More scans = more aspects = more research
2. Keep multiple wands - they recharge slowly
3. Build your workshop near magical trees (Silverwood/Greatwood)
4. Start research early - some require specific aspects
5. Be careful with Eldritch knowledge - it has consequences
6. Use Flux Scrubbers to prevent magical pollution
7. Thaumium tools are better than diamond in some ways
8. Nitor makes permanent light sources
9. Save Primordial Pearls for important crafting
10. The Thaumonomicon is your friend - read it!

## New Feature: Wand Spell System

The wand is no longer just for crafting! Cast powerful combat spells:

### Spell Categories
1. **Offensive Spells** (6 spells)
   - Fireball: Explosive projectile
   - Zap: Lightning strike
   - Ice Shard: Freezing projectile
   - Explosion: Massive TNT blast
   - Void Vortex: Pull and damage enemies
   - Shockwave: Area knockback attack

2. **Defensive Spells** (2 spells)
   - Shield: Damage resistance + absorption
   - Heal: Restore health + regeneration

3. **Mobility Spells** (2 spells)
   - Blink: Instant teleport forward
   - Leap: Launch into the air

### Key Features
- **Vis-based casting**: Each spell costs Vis (15-40)
- **Cooldown system**: Prevents spell spam (1.5-5 seconds)
- **Spell cycling**: Shift+Right-Click to switch spells
- **Auto-recharge**: Vis regenerates 1 per second
- **Visual feedback**: Durability bar shows Vis level
