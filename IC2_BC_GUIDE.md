# IndustrialCraft & BuildCraft Integration Guide

## Overview
ThaumVibe integrates classic IndustrialCraft 2 (IC2) and BuildCraft (BC) features, allowing players to combine magical and technological systems for enhanced automation and power generation.

## IndustrialCraft 2 Features

### Power System: EU (Energy Units)

#### What is EU?
EU (Energy Units) is the electrical power system used by IndustrialCraft machines. Energy is generated, stored, and consumed in EU.

#### Energy Storage Tiers
1. **BatBox**: 40,000 EU capacity - Basic energy storage
2. **MFE**: 600,000 EU capacity - Medium voltage storage
3. **MFSU**: 10,000,000 EU capacity - High voltage storage

#### Energy Generation
- **Generator**: Burns fuel items to produce EU
- **Solar Panel**: Generates EU from sunlight (1 EU/t during day)
- **Geothermal Generator**: Uses lava to generate EU
- **Nuclear Reactor**: Advanced, high-output power generation (dangerous!)

#### Energy Transmission
- **Copper Cable**: Basic energy transmission (low loss)
- **Gold Cable**: Medium energy transmission (very low loss)
- **Glass Fiber Cable**: Advanced energy transmission (minimal loss, long distance)

### Ores and Materials

#### Ores
- **Copper Ore**: Common ore, smelts into Copper Ingot
- **Tin Ore**: Common ore, smelts into Tin Ingot
- **Uranium Ore**: Rare ore for nuclear applications
- **Deepslate Variants**: Found deeper underground, harder to mine

#### Metals and Alloys
- **Copper Ingot**: Used in cables and bronze production
- **Tin Ingot**: Used in batteries and bronze production
- **Bronze Ingot**: Alloy of copper and tin (stronger than iron)
- **Steel Ingot**: Advanced metal for high-tier machines
- **Uranium Ingot**: Nuclear fuel
- **Refined Iron**: Purified iron for advanced components

#### Dusts
Process ores in the **Macerator** to get dusts:
- **Iron Dust**: 1 Iron Ore → 2 Iron Dust (double output!)
- **Gold Dust**: 1 Gold Ore → 2 Gold Dust
- **Copper Dust**: 1 Copper Ore → 2 Copper Dust
- **Tin Dust**: 1 Tin Ore → 2 Tin Dust
- **Bronze Dust**: Crafted from copper and tin dust

Smelt dusts to get ingots - this effectively doubles your ore output!

### Machines

#### Processing Machines
1. **Macerator**
   - Grinds ores into dust
   - Doubles ore output when combined with smelting
   - Can process various items into powders
   - Requires EU to operate

2. **Extractor**
   - Extracts rubber from resin
   - Processes items to extract components
   - Requires EU to operate

3. **Compressor**
   - Compresses materials into denser forms
   - Creates plates and advanced components
   - Requires EU to operate

4. **Electric Furnace**
   - Faster than regular furnace
   - Uses EU instead of fuel
   - No smelting time when fully powered

5. **Recycler**
   - Converts unwanted items into scrap
   - Scrap can be used in advanced crafting
   - Requires EU to operate

### Components

#### Circuits
- **Electronic Circuit**: Basic circuit for machine crafting
- **Advanced Circuit**: High-tier circuit for advanced machines

#### Energy Storage Items
- **RE-Battery**: Portable battery, stores 10,000 EU
- **Advanced RE-Battery**: Stores 100,000 EU
- **Energy Crystal**: Stores 1,000,000 EU
- **Lapotron Crystal**: Highest capacity, stores 10,000,000 EU

### Progression Path

#### Early IC2
1. Mine Copper and Tin ores
2. Craft basic cables and circuits
3. Build a Generator for power
4. Create your first battery
5. Construct a BatBox for energy storage

#### Mid IC2
1. Build a Macerator to double ore output
2. Set up Solar Panels for renewable energy
3. Craft advanced circuits
4. Construct an MFE for more storage
5. Build Electric Furnace for fast smelting

#### Late IC2
1. Find Uranium Ore
2. Create Energy Crystals
3. Build an MFSU for massive storage
4. Construct a Nuclear Reactor (carefully!)
5. Use Lapotron Crystals for ultimate energy storage

## BuildCraft Features

### Power System: MJ (Minecraft Joules)

#### What is MJ?
MJ (Minecraft Joules) is the mechanical power system used by BuildCraft. It's generated by engines and transmitted through pipes.

#### Engines (Power Generation)
1. **Stirling Engine**
   - Burns solid fuels (coal, wood)
   - Basic, reliable power source
   - Produces 1 MJ/t

2. **Combustion Engine**
   - Burns liquid fuels (oil, fuel)
   - High power output
   - Produces 5 MJ/t
   - Requires cooling!

3. **Steam Engine**
   - Uses steam to generate power
   - Medium power output
   - Produces 2 MJ/t
   - Requires water and heat

#### Power Transmission
- **Wooden Power Pipe**: Extracts power from engines
- **Cobblestone Power Pipe**: Basic power transmission
- **Stone Power Pipe**: Improved power transmission
- **Gold Power Pipe**: Fast power transmission

### Automation and Machines

#### Mining and Resource Gathering
1. **Quarry**
   - Automatically mines a large area
   - Requires significant MJ power
   - Extracts all blocks in marked area
   - Stores items in adjacent chests

2. **Pump**
   - Extracts fluids from the world
   - Works on lakes and oceans
   - Requires MJ power
   - Can be connected to fluid pipes

#### Building and Automation
1. **Filler**
   - Automatically fills or clears areas
   - Multiple pattern modes
   - Requires MJ power
   - Great for terraforming

2. **Builder**
   - Constructs structures from blueprints
   - Can copy and paste buildings
   - Requires MJ power
   - Advanced automation tool

#### Crafting Stations
1. **Assembly Table**
   - Crafts complex items
   - Uses laser energy
   - Required for advanced recipes
   - Programmable crafting

2. **Advanced Crafting Table**
   - Enhanced crafting table
   - Stores crafting patterns
   - Remembers last recipe
   - No power required

### Transport Systems

#### Item Transport Pipes
- **Wooden Transport Pipe**: Extracts items from inventories
- **Cobblestone Transport Pipe**: Basic item transport
- **Stone Transport Pipe**: Improved transport speed
- **Iron Transport Pipe**: Directional routing
- **Golden Transport Pipe**: High-speed item transport
- **Diamond Transport Pipe**: Filtering and sorting

#### Fluid Transport Pipes
- **Wooden Fluid Pipe**: Extracts fluids from tanks
- **Cobblestone Fluid Pipe**: Basic fluid transport
- **Stone Fluid Pipe**: Improved fluid transport

### Components

#### Gears
Essential crafting components for BuildCraft items:
- **Wooden Gear**: Basic gear for simple items
- **Stone Gear**: Improved gear for engines
- **Iron Gear**: Strong gear for machines
- **Gold Gear**: Advanced gear for complex machinery
- **Diamond Gear**: High-tier gear for advanced machines

### Storage

#### Tank
- Stores large amounts of fluids
- Can be stacked vertically for more capacity
- Glass construction allows visual inspection
- Connects to fluid pipes

### Progression Path

#### Early BC

2. Build a Stirling Engine
3. Create basic transport pipes
4. Set up simple item automation
5. Build a Tank for fluid storage

#### Mid BC
1. Craft iron and gold gears
2. Build a Combustion Engine
3. Set up an automated mining system
4. Create fluid transport networks
5. Build an Advanced Crafting Table

#### Late BC
1. Craft diamond gears
2. Build a Quarry for automatic mining
3. Set up complex pipe networks
4. Use Assembly Table for advanced items
5. Create automated building systems

## Tips and Tricks

### IndustrialCraft Tips
1. Always use the Macerator before smelting ores - doubles output!
2. Solar Panels are slow but infinite - build arrays
3. Glass Fiber Cable has minimal loss - use for long distances
4. Nuclear Reactors are powerful but dangerous - plan carefully
5. Keep spare batteries charged for portable power

### BuildCraft Tips
1. Wooden pipes extract, all others transport
2. Iron pipes direct items in one direction only
3. Diamond pipes can filter and sort automatically
4. Engines need to warm up for full power output
5. Combustion engines MUST be cooled or they explode!

### Integration Tips
1. Scan everything for aspects - tech items have unique aspects
2. Set up dedicated power systems for different needs
3. Use pipes to automate all crafting stations
4. Build centralized power generation facilities
5. Plan your base layout for efficient resource flow

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues
- **Machine not working**: Check power connection and EU storage
- **Pipes not connecting**: Ensure correct pipe types
- **Engine overheating**: Add cooling (water) to combustion engines
- **Quarry not mining**: Check MJ power supply and marked area
- **Items not routing**: Check diamond pipe filters

### Power Management
- Monitor energy storage levels
- Use multiple power sources for reliability
- Keep backup power systems
- Balance consumption and generation
- Upgrade storage as you expand
